Who We Are

ClearSide Exteriors, LLC

ClearSide Exteriors was founded by a North Shore student who saw an opportunity to serve his community while learning the value of building something from scratch. What started as a summer hustle with a bucket and a squeegee has grown into a trusted local business — 600+ homes serviced — built on quality, reliability, and genuine care for every customer.

From day one, the mission has been simple: show up, do exceptional work, and treat every home and business like it's our own. No cutting corners, no generic results — just the kind of attention to detail that keeps customers coming back season after season.

  • North Shore student-founded & operated
  • Based on the North Shore, serving Cook County
  • 4+ years in business and growing
  • Locally owned, community focused
